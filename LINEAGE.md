This project exists because people have already burned time, compute, tokens, and sanity figuring things out.

Research has a cost: reading code, writing code, digging through issues, getting dead ends, and increasingly, burning tokens asking machines the right (or wrong) questions to automate all of this.

If this project saves you some of that work, great! That's the point.

Do whatever you want with the project: fork it, rewrite it, break it, sell it, or turn it into something else.

If you reuse work from here, consider leaving a reference to where it came from, or tell your clanker to write it down for you. Tokens are now the real cost of research and building, so better recycle the research and leave a trail so others can find the original architecture, references and prior work.
